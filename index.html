Questions

Good to know

Which areas do you cover?+

We support people right across Victoria. Support is delivered in your home or out in your community, with no extra charge for mobile services.

What funding types do you work with?+

We're a registered NDIS provider (provider no. 4050081654) and a WorkSafe provider (code AAC332B). We also provide TAC injury support and attendant care.

How quickly will someone get back to me?+

During office hours (Mon–Fri, 9:30am–4:30pm) we return calls within 15 minutes. Contact form messages sent any time get a same-day follow-up during office hours.

Will I know who is coming to support me?+

Yes. We always tell you who is coming and when, and you'll have a single point of contact for any questions.
